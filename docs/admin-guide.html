Every training day

  • Open Coach check-in so coaches scan the QR

Every week

  • Open Reports → Weekly report

  • Open Month-end report (the dashboard reminds you)

  • Collect money still owed
  • Check the month's expenses

Signing in, language, phone app

Sign in

  1. Open the app link.
  2. Type the admin PIN and tap Login.
First time: the admin PIN starts as 0000. Change it straight away in Settings → Access & messages. It can't be the same as a coach code.

Switch language or theme

Tap عربي / EN at the top for Arabic or English. The sun/moon button switches light and dark mode.

Install it on your phone like an app

  • Android (Chrome): tap Install on the bar at the bottom.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

It opens full screen with the Wolves icon. It needs internet. With no connection it shows "No internet" and never shows old numbers.

Share the link with coaches

Tap the QR icon at the top (Access QR). Coaches scan it to open the app, then sign in with their own code.

Players

Menu → Players

Add a player

  1. Tap + Add player.
  2. Fill in first, middle and last name, date of birth, gender and school.
  3. Add the father's name and phone and the mother's name and phone. Phones are 07XXXXXXXX.
  4. Pick the group. The app suggests one from age and gender.
  5. Leave "trial used" unchecked to give a free first session. It's used automatically the first time they're marked present.
  6. Save. You can start their package straight away.

Find and sort players

  • Search by name or phone.
  • Filter by school: each school shows how many players come from it.
  • Sort by name, school, group or age.
  • Filter by group or status (active, frozen, left, pending).

A player's page (tap any player)

  • Package progress: sessions used and left, end date.
  • Payments and receipts, attendance history, both parents' contacts.
  • Edit details, Renew the package, Record payment.
  • WhatsApp the parent. If the father's number is missing, it goes to the mother.

Freeze a player (injury, travel)

  1. Open the player → Freeze → pick the start date.
  2. While frozen, their sessions don't count and the package moves forward.
  3. When they're back: Unfreeze → pick the return date.

Approve players added by coaches

Coaches can add a new kid at training. The kid shows as Pending on the Dashboard. Approve to make them a real player, or reject.

4

Packages and payments

How a package works: 12 training sessions on the calendar. A session is used when its date passes, whether the player came or not. Cancelled days and frozen days don't count.

Start or renew a package

  1. Open the player → Renew.
  2. Pick the start date and package. The app shows the end date.
  3. Enter what was paid now. Less than the price = the rest is owed. 0 = nothing paid yet.

Record a payment (or the rest of a balance)

  1. Player → Record payment.
  2. Amount, method (cash / CliQ), date → Confirm, payment received.
  3. A receipt (numbered WSA-…) is made. Print it or send it.

Fix a mistake

On the player's page you can edit or delete a payment or a package.

All payments

Menu → Payments

Every payment by month and method, with receipts. Tap Excel to download the list.

Attendance

Menu → Attendance

Take attendance

  1. Pick the group at the top.
  2. Check the date (arrows, or tap Today).
  3. Tap each player: present / absent. Or tap Mark all present.
  4. Tap Close session for the summary you can copy to WhatsApp.

Open the group's WhatsApp

When a group is picked, the green WhatsApp icon next to "Mark all present" opens that group's chat (if you added the link, see section 9).

Cancel a training day (rain, Eid, court closed)

  1. Go to that date → tap Cancel this session.
  2. Optional: type the reason ("Rain").
  3. Confirm. Nobody loses a session: every package moves forward one session. Marks already taken that day are removed, and a used free trial is given back.

Mark a coach present by hand

On the attendance page, the coaches strip lets you tap a coach to mark them present for that date. Tap again to undo.

A player who trains without an active package is flagged as an unpaid session and shows in "money owed".

Coaches and check-in

Menu → Coaches

Add a coach

  1. Tap + Add coach.
  2. Name, phone, salary type: monthly (fixed) or per session (paid for each day worked), and the amount.
  3. Login code: type 4 to 8 digits, or leave it empty and a code is made for you.
  4. Save. Give the coach their code. They sign in with it.

See a coach's activity

Tap a coach to see every login (date and time), the days they worked, how many attendance marks they took, and their salary this month.

Stop a coach's access

Edit the coach → untick Active, or change their code. They are signed out immediately.

Coach check-in: 2 ways (this is what salaries count)

Coaches → Coach check-in
  • Way 1: QR. Keep this page open on your phone or laptop. The coach scans the QR, signs in if needed, and taps I'm on court. The code changes every minute, so a photo of it can't be used later from home.
  • Way 2: WhatsApp link. Tap the green WhatsApp button next to a coach. WhatsApp opens with a message and a link. The coach taps the link, then I'm on court. No login needed. The link works once, today only.

The page shows live who is in, at what time, and how (QR / WhatsApp / by admin).

Coaches can't check themselves in any other way, and marking players does not check them in. If a coach forgot, mark them present by hand on the Attendance page.
7

Salaries

How much each coach gets

  • Monthly coach: the fixed amount.
  • Per-session coach: days checked in × rate.

Pay a salary

  1. Open Month-end report (or Finance → coaches).
  2. Each coach shows Due, Paid and what's left.
  3. Tap Pay X JD. The status turns to Paid. Part payments work too.
Any salary not yet handed out stays on the list as owed to coaches until it's paid.

Finance and expenses

Menu → Finance

The money picture

For any month: money in (payments plus event and sponsorship income), money out (salaries plus expenses), profit, cash in the box, a weekly chart, and where the month will land.

Add an expense

  1. Tap Add expense → amount, category, date, note.
  2. Choose the type: one time, every month (rent), or per session (court booking).
  3. To end a monthly expense: Stop from today. Resume brings it back.

Log today's session costs

Finance or Dashboard → Session costs: court bookings (with or without lights) and water, using the prices set in Settings.

Copy last month's expenses

One tap copies last month's one-time expenses into this month.

9

Groups

Menu → Groups

Create or edit a group

  1. Tap + Add group, or tap a group to edit it.
  2. Arabic and English name, ages from / to, boys / girls / mixed, training days, time.
  3. WhatsApp group link (optional): in WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Business) open the group → Group info → Invite via link → Copy. Paste it here.
  4. Save. A green WhatsApp icon appears on the group, and on Attendance when that group is picked. Tap it to jump into the group chat.
A group with players in it can't be deleted. Move the players first.

Events

Menu → Events

Add an event

  1. Tap Add event.
  2. Type: school visit, outdoor, indoor, tournament, collab, sponsorship, other.
  3. Date, place, note, and an amount if it brings money (sponsorship, fees). That amount counts as income in Finance.

Excel, backups, AI assistant

Excel

  • Export everything: Dashboard or Players → Export all.
  • Import players: Players → download the template, fill it in, upload it.

Backups

Online, the database (Supabase) is backed up automatically. On the laptop, a backup copy is made every day.

AI assistant

Ask questions in English or Arabic like "who is frozen?", "who owes money?", "whose package ends this week?" and get the answer with links.

What coaches can and can't do

Coaches CANCoaches CAN'T
  • Sign in with their own code
  • Check in by scanning your QR or tapping your WhatsApp link
  • Take attendance for any group
  • Add a new kid at training (waits for your approval)
  • Open the group's WhatsApp
  • See money, payments, finance or salaries
  • Check themselves in without your QR or link
  • Mark other coaches present
  • Cancel a training day
  • Change settings, groups, prices or codes
Every attendance mark records which coach took it, and every login is saved with the date and time.
